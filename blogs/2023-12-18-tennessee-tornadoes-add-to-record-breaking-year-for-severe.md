The deadly tornadoes that tore through Tennessee on December 9th are likely to become the 20th billion-dollar severe storm-related event of 2023, adding to a record-breaking year for the frequency and costliness of severe convective storms (SCS). As of December 8, The National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) estimates the aggregate damage cost of SCS events in the US for 2023 to be a staggering $52.7 Billion.
