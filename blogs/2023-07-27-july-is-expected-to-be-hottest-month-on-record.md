July 27th, 2023 – The World Meteorological Organization and the EU's Copernicus Climate Change Service reported on Thursday that July 2023 is expected to be Earth's hottest month on record, following on from the hottest June on record. The average global mean surface air temperature for the first 23 days of July was 16.95°C, breaking the previous record of 16.63°C from July 2019.
