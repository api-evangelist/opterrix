Tampa, Florida – September 12, 2023 – American Integrity Insurance Group , a premier Tampa-based property insurance carrier, announced today that it has partnered with Opterrix™, a leader in proactive risk intelligence for the property and casualty insurance industry, to strengthen its underwriting processes, and risk management strategies.
